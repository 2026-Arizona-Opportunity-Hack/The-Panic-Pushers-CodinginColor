03 · Built for Coding in Color

Developing Arizona's future digital workforce.

Coding in Color brings computer science and AI to Arizona high schoolers whose schools don't offer it: AI Academy cohorts at Desert Edge, Maryvale and Salt River, year-long programs, summer camps and Day of Compute. This classroom turns those lessons into something any teacher can run from a link, and shows how much students actually learned.
